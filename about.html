My TaeKwonDo story

I was a rising TaeKwonDo prospect on track for the national team.

2
National podiums
4th
Thailand Championship, cadet
3
Countries coached

Two national podiums came first: runner-up at the 33rd National Youth Games in Chumporn-Ranong and at the 35th Kanchanaburi Games, then 4th place at the Thailand Championship in the cadet category.

A left-knee injury ended that run before I made the national team.

I stayed in sport. I represented Bangkok University at the university games, played provincial-level basketball for Lopburi, and worked as an assistant coach with teams from Singapore, China and Taiwan.

Losing that shot taught me what I still use in engineering: rebuild after a setback, keep training the basics, and make the team around you better.
