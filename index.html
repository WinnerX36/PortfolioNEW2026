About

Software engineer — AI systems, internal platforms and the web.

I build software that has to hold up in production.

Most of my work sits between the model and the product: an OCR pipeline that reads Thai invoices, a platform that replaced four internal tools, STQ Orbit where accounting data is corrected and validated before it reaches QuickBooks and receipts are issued automatically, a chatbot that answers with sources instead of guesses, and a reasoning model I trained myself on Thai data — then published as open weights.

I care about the unglamorous parts — response time under load, inference cost per document, a schema that still makes sense a year later, and an interface that loads on a slow connection.
