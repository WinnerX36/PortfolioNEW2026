FAQ

Who is Wuttiphong Butta (วุฒิพงษ์ บุตตา)?

Wuttiphong Butta (Thai: วุฒิพงษ์ บุตตา, nickname โพสต์อิท) is a software engineer in Bangkok, Thailand. He is a Computer Science graduate of Bangkok University (2026) and works as AI & Technology Specialist at Give Education, building full-stack and AI systems.

What does Wuttiphong Butta build?

He builds production web apps and AI systems end to end: a billing and cash-tracking platform with QuickBooks sync for Aquatots Thailand, an AI OCR system for Thai invoices, a LINE OA chatbot, and QwenMythos, a 4B open-weight reasoning model. His stack includes React, Next.js, Node.js, FastAPI, Docker and AWS.

What is QwenMythos?

QwenMythos is a 4B-parameter open-weight reasoning model built by Wuttiphong Butta. It uses a looped (Recurrent-Depth) transformer fine-tuned from a Qwen base on a curated Thai reasoning dataset, and ships as safetensors and a Q4_K_M GGUF that runs locally with llama.cpp or Ollama.

What was the AI OCR project at Smarterware?

During his internship at Smarterware Co., Ltd. (a member of SAMART TELCOMS), Wuttiphong fine-tuned Typhoon OCR with LoRA adapters through Unsloth. The system reached 98.88% text-extraction accuracy on Thai invoices, cut manual processing time by 80%, and was deployed with FastAPI, Docker and AWS SageMaker.

What is Ruvion-Nok Daeng?

Ruvion-Nok Daeng is a startup founded by Wuttiphong Butta. Its website is ruvionnokdaeng.vercel.app.

Does Wuttiphong Butta offer SEO, GEO and AEO work?

Yes. He works as a freelance consultant on technical SEO, GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AEO (answer engine optimisation): audits, structured data and AI search intelligence tools. He takes freelance work through Fastwork and by email.

What is Wuttiphong Butta's sports background?

He was a rising TaeKwonDo prospect with two national podiums (runner-up at the 33rd National Youth Games and the 35th Kanchanaburi Games) and 4th place at the Thailand Championship (cadet). A left-knee injury ended his shot at the national team. He later represented Bangkok University and Lopburi province in basketball, and coached alongside teams from Singapore, China and Taiwan.
